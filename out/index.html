How the rating works

Each contest gives every participant a performance in [0, 4000]: perf = 4000 × (N − rank + 241) / (N + 240) × score / max score, where N is the number of participants. The best participant always gets 4000.

A season's performances are sorted from best to worst and weighted with Wi = max(0.01, 0.1 × 0.95i−1). The i-th external participation (a contest missed for another training or competition) counts as the i(i+1)/2-th best actual performance.

ratings = seasonReset(ratings−1) + Σ Wi × Pi with seasonReset(x) = xlog4000 400 (so 4000 carries over as 400). A rating never decreases within a season.
